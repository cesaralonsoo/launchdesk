    Questions

    Is the research real?

    On paid runs, yes: analysts read the web and cite every source. The tour and practice runs use sample data and say so.

    What if the verdict is No-go?

    You keep the report. A No-go for 10¢ is cheaper than finding out in six months. Try another idea.

    Why Bitcoin Cash?

    A 10¢ payment only works when the fee is a fraction of a cent, and the escrow is a contract anyone can check.

    Which wallet do I need?

    Any BCH wallet that scans a QR or supports WalletConnect. You can read the finished example without one.
