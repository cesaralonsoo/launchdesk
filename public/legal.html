Terms and privacy

The short version: no accounts, no tracking, payments straight from your wallet, and AI output you should check before you rely on it.

What we store

  • Your idea and what the team made from it: the text you typed, the plan, report, numbers, brand and launch kit. They are kept so your project link keeps working.
  • Payments: the stage, the amount, the transaction id and the time. Transactions on Bitcoin Cash are public by design.
  • Your public page, if you build one: its name, copy, logo and the Bitcoin Cash address that receives pre-orders.
  • In your browser only: the list under "My projects", a private key that proves a project is yours, and your sound setting. Clearing your browser data removes them.

We don't ask for your name or email, we don't use advertising or analytics trackers, and we don't sell anything about you.

To write the results, your idea is sent to an AI model provider (Anthropic). Anyone with a project link can open that project, so share links with care.

Payments

  • You pay per stage in Bitcoin Cash, straight from your own wallet. LaunchDesk never holds your keys.
  • Prices are set in US dollars and converted at the rate shown when you pay. Your wallet sets the network fee, usually under $0.01.
  • Money moving between rooms in the office is internal, not on-chain: it shows how a stage's budget is split, nothing more.
  • If a step fails after you paid, running it again on the same project is free.
  • Pre-orders on a public page go straight to that founder's address. LaunchDesk never holds those funds and can't refund them; the founder can.
  • On a demo server nothing is charged, and every demo screen says so.

AI output

Everything the team produces is written by AI. It is guidance, not financial or legal advice. Numbers marked "Estimate" are estimates, not research. Names are not checked for trademarks. Check anything important with a professional before you spend money on it.

You own what LaunchDesk makes for you. Don't use it to publish pages that mislead people or break the law: such pages can be taken down.

LaunchDesk is an open-source project built for the BCH BLAZE 2026 hackathon. The service is provided as is, without warranties.
